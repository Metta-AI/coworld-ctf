Paintbot League · Season 2

The Cheat Sheet

Every rank, every deed, every achievement — and exactly what each one pays.

Ranking up pays power.

XP is yours alone, earned by work, and it makes your cog stronger. A rank-up pays zero GLORY — the pop shows stars, because the power is the whole prize.

Deeds pay GLORY.

GLORY is the team score the crowd watches. It comes from deeds and achievements — outcomes, on camera — and only GLORY wins the broadcast.

I · Ranking Up

Your rank lives and dies with your current life. Work fills the bar; each rank buys a new capability, not just a bigger number.

The fine print. Ranks reset on death — the whole ladder, buffs and all, and a fallen Ironhide-or-better also hands the enemy that 40-GLORY bounty. Your rank never drops while you live, unless you paint a teammate. And the Ironhide HP is headroom, not a heal: the extra hit point must still be earned back from a med kit.

The supply drop — rank 3+ turns your work into team kit

Once you hit Ironhide, every 20 fresh XP you earn makes your team's heart spit out a pickup, cycling med kit → paint-bomb → spray can → shield. At most one drop per 90 ticks and 4 per life — and it is fed by earning, never by standing around: a veteran who hides produces exactly nothing.

The three multipliers — applied in this order; penalties skip all three

Where ×1.5

Ground ownership at the deed site. Your own ground pays face value — defending your keep is table stakes. A deed landed on enemy ground pays ×1.5: initiative into defended territory.

Heat ×2 / ×4 / ×8

Each heat deed adds an ember to your team's streak: 2 embers light ×2, 5 light ×4, 10 light ×8. Go quiet for ~2 seconds and you shed 2 embers per window — a maxed streak cools off in ~11 seconds. Achievements never climb heat.

Carrying ×2

While your team holds the enemy heart, every heat deed pays double. A passive carrier earns nothing; a carrying team that fights earns twice.

III · The Achievement Book

Eight trees, five tiers each. Every tier is a one-time team claim per game — tier I pays 9, then 11, 14, 18, and 23 GLORY at tier V. The where multiplier applies; heat never does. And tier V carries the pioneer bonus: the first team in the game to claim it banks ×3 — 69 GLORY.
