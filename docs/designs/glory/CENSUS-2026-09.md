## Method

1. `tools/glory/discover_cohort.py` walked `/v2/rounds` (paging via
   `cursor`, not `next_cursor` — see script) and resolved each distinct
   `coworld_version`'s `manifest.game.runnable.source_url` to a commit,
   then `git diff` on the three scoring-relevant source files bucketed
   commits into eras.
2. Built two `extract_events` binaries at permanent, non-repo locations (Nim bakes
   the source path at compile time — never build in `/tmp`):
   `census_gv59_gv15_build` (commit `61b6ab79`,
   GameVersion 59) and `census_gv60_build` (commit `29af1ad0`, GameVersion
   60, identical scoring to 17509ef6). Both are exactly GloryVersion 15.
3. `tools/glory/census_decode.py` downloaded and re-simulated all 305
   replays, reconstructing each seat's recut product score: seed 1, fold
   every `glory_deed`/`achievement` event's `amount` when `>1`, divide by
   `2^(friendly-fire halvings)`, apply the win factor (×8, solo) at
   finalize, cap at `2^24`.
4. **Validated against the platform's own `participant_scores` before
   trusting anything downstream: 4,880/4,880 (100.00%) seat-episodes
   reconciled exactly.** (First pass reconciled 0/4,880 — achievement
   events fold into the *same* product as deed events, e.g. `treeSquad`
   Clean Sheet mints amount=2 for every seat every episode; the first
   draft only multiplied glory_deed amounts. Caught by the reconciliation
   check itself, exactly the discipline the `coworld.measure` skill
   prescribes.)
5. `tools/glory/census_analyze.py` produced the tables below.

## Q4 — Recipe share (top-decile seat-episodes, n=509, threshold=576)

- `closing_time` fires (amount>1) in **82.5%** of top-decile seat-episodes;
  `jointact` fires in **2.8%**; the seat is the episode's winner in
  **53.0%**.
- All three (closing_time>1 AND jointact>1 AND win) fire **together** in
  only **3/509 (0.59%)** of top-decile seat-episodes — the "closing-time ×
  joint-act × win = 192" recipe from the discussion brief is NOT the
  dominant top-decile shape today; it's a rare full-stack, not the modal
  jackpot. Note: `dClosingTime` mints repeatedly per episode (28.5% of
  seats who get it at all get it ≥2 times), so its contribution compounds
  — measured as a running product per seat, not a single fold.
  `dJointAct` is now pact-gated (GloryVersion 15) and correspondingly rare
  (7.21% of episodes overall, Q1).
- **recipe(closing_time × jointact × win8) explains a mean 42.2% / median
  44.6% of a top-decile score's log2-magnitude** (n=451 with recipe>1) —
  i.e., on average the named "recipe" carriers explain under half the
  bit-length of a top round; the rest is achievement stacking
  (`treeGun`/`treeSquad`), `dFirstBlood`/`dAceTag`/`dLongshotKill` class
  factors, and the placement ladder (`dFinal8/4/2`, which fires in 100% of
  episodes and is NOT part of the named recipe but is a real, near-
  universal multiplicative floor — see Q1).

### Heat rung distribution (seat-time share, tick-weighted, full episode; n=4,880 seat-episodes, 13,236,256 seat-ticks)

| rung | share of seat-time |
|---|---:|
| x1 | 96.8092% |
| x2 | 2.3273% |
| x4 | 0.8512% |
| x8 | 0.0122% |

Heat is **no longer fully dead** (the GV56-era measurement was
>x1 only 0.08–0.26%, x4/x8 never observed) — the `[1,2,4]` threshold /
270-tick decay retune (HEAT ARM E) is measurably working: x2 now occurs
1.3% of the time predicted by the old law's counterfactual estimate off
by roughly an order of magnitude below the pre-registered counterfactual
(76.5/19.5/3.8/0.1% for x1/x2/x4/x8) — the counterfactual was computed by
replaying OLD data through the NEW constants and evidently over-predicted;
this is the first LIVE re-measurement under the real constants and real
play, and it reads far cooler than the counterfactual. x8 is genuinely
rare (0.0122%, ~1,620 of 13.2M seat-ticks) — "near-mythic" is still an
accurate description. Modeling note: heat state is walked per seat over
the FULL episode length (not cut at the seat's own death), because
`sim.heatEmbers` is TEAM state and a downed-mode kill credit can mint
*after* its own killer has already died (directly observed: seat 2 of
episode `ereq_b19bc577` died at tick 1754, minted a heat-paying
`dLongshotKill` at tick 1883). Cutting at death would silently drop these
delayed-finalize updates.

## What is NOT verified

- **p99.9 is a ~5-sample estimate.** Real, not fabricated, but do not treat
  it as stable — a single future outlier round could move it substantially.
- **Round-count target (200) missed** — 24 rounds is the entire existing
  population at this GloryVersion; more data requires either waiting on
  the live ladder (main has since moved to GV16, so the window may be
  closing) or accepting a mixed-era sample (not done here, by design).
- **LONGSHOT's exact gunRange per sampled episode is unconfirmed** (see
  Q5) — the extractor doesn't expose it; the mint-rate measurement itself
  is unaffected.
- **Heat-occupancy denominator choice** (full episode vs. alive-only) is a
  modeling decision, documented above and in `tools/glory/README.md`; an
  alive-only cut understates x2+ occupancy for reasons explained there.
- Two additional same-version (0.7.367) rounds, r4540–r4541, completed
  after this census's data pull and are NOT included in the sample (would
  add ~2 rounds/~32 episodes if re-run before a GV16 build ships).
- This is a whole-field census (all 16 seats/episode), not specific to any
  one policy — no name-prefix attribution was made or needed.
