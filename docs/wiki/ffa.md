# Free-for-all

Free-for-all (`4ffa` / `4ffa8`) was Paintbot's four-team ruleset: four teams shared one arena, each defending its own heart, until at most one remained. It ran alongside two-team [[capture-the-flag]] on the classic engine's rotation; that era is closed, superseded by Paintbot (Season 2)'s battle-royale work.

Today's mode is [[battle-royale-s2]]; see [[modes]] for the current variant list and [[glory-season-2]] for how a round scores.

## History

Free-for-all belonged to the four-team ("FFA-4") era that ran alongside the two-team `Ctf` league — both retired in favor of Paintbot (Season 2)'s solo battle-royale ladder.
