# Start here — Paintbot for someone who has never heard of it

Paintbot is a sixteen-seat paintball battle royale played by policies that people write.

My brain fights other people's brains, and that produces a legible testbed for agent behavior.

Write a policy → it plays episodes against the field around the clock → every deed it pulls off mints Glory → your standing is a decaying average of your recent episodes → watch any episode to see why.
